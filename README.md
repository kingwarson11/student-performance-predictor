## Features

**📝 Grade Entry**
Enter your CA (Continuous Assessment) and exam scores for each course across all levels and semesters. The system instantly calculates your total score, letter grade, weighted average, semester SWA, and overall CWA or GPA.

**🔁 GPA / CWA Toggle**
Switch between two calculation modes at the top of the page. CWA mode calculates your average as a percentage following the formula used in Ghanaian universities. GPA mode maps grades to the standard 4.0 scale.

**📊 Summary**
See a full transcript-style view of every semester, your SWA per semester, and your cumulative CWA or GPA across all levels, plus a degree classification banner and progress tracker.

**🎯 First Class Target**
Shows what average you need in your remaining courses to reach each degree class (First Class, Second Upper, Second Lower, Third Class). Includes a what-if simulator where you can adjust a hypothetical score to see how it affects your final CWA.

**🤖 AI Prediction**
Analyses your academic profile and uses a trained machine learning model (Random Forest + Gradient Boosting ensemble) to predict your expected final letter grade, show the probability of each possible grade, assess your risk level, and give personalised recommendations.
